# Milestone 4 Review — Traditional Game AI ("a living world")

> QA Engineer and Critical Reviewer · 2026-11-23 · No generative AI anywhere: everything below is conventional, deterministic game AI.

## Delivered (3 feature commits)

1. **Living NPCs**:
   - a behaviour-tree runtime with serialisable running state;
   - daily routines per archetype: schedule blocks plus utility scoring over hunger, fatigue and social need, with momentum;
   - activities executed as behaviour trees: sleep and eat indoors, work at a stall, workshop or office, Saints patrol their turf, friends meet and chat;
   - memory: first-hand records, hourly decay, important memories that never fade, and line-of-sight perception;
   - gossip turns memories into rumours that travel between NPCs;
   - relationships (trust, fear, affection, respect) derived from memory drive dialogue and street reactions;
   - a background crowd whose density follows the clock, and distance level-of-detail for far NPCs;
   - an F3 AI debug overlay.
2. **Factions and world director**:
   - GOAP planning over symbolic facts for utility-chosen goals;
   - multi-hour operations with visible crews that you can disrupt;
   - retaliation after losing a block;
   - Markov weather;
   - a world-event table with time windows (blackout, night market, recall drive, procession), and both factions react to events;
   - rain, storm and blackout rendering; dead signals during blackouts.
3. **Procedural missions**:
   - 5 deterministic templates bound to live NPCs, buildings, species and faction operations, with completability validation;
   - Co-op job board and NPC requests ("!" markers); deadlines and failure;
   - new objective kinds (capture, disrupt);
   - **objective waypoints**: an in-world marker, an off-screen edge arrow with distance, and "near ⟨street⟩". This came from playtest feedback that players had to try every house.

## Bugs found and fixed

- **Battle freeze** (also behind the flaky e2e capture step): a failed capture destroyed the snare while a delayed tween still targeted it, throwing inside Phaser's frame update and stalling the battle UI. Fixed by binding animations to their own objects; the e2e test now prints diagnostics if it ever recurs.
- The GOAP tick crashed when a goal was already satisfied (empty plan). Fixed and tested.
- Traffic signals stayed lit during blackouts; now they go dark and junctions become unsignalled.

## Critical review

- *GOAP for two factions with about five actions each* is borderline overkill, but it's what makes plans change with circumstances (blackout → accumulate, block lost → raid) without hand-written branching, and adding the other three factions is now data.
- *Memory → relationship is derived, not stored.* Simpler and always consistent; the cost is a small fold per query, which is negligible.
- *The utility AI picks one activity at a time; no multi-goal HSM layers yet.* Threat responses (flee, confront) still pre-empt routines via modes, which covers what players see.
- *The crowd is transient.* Background pedestrians don't persist memories across despawns. They're intentionally unimportant; named NPCs carry the long-term memory.
