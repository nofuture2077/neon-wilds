# Milestone 2 Review — Creature Gameplay

> QA Engineer and Critical Reviewer · 2026-11-23

## Delivered

- **Type system:** 8 elements with the GDD chart, dual-type multiplication and environment modifiers (night → Shade, park → Bloom, neon street → Spark, alley → Toxic).
- **11 species** (10 wild-catchable in the Slums plus the story Glitchling), 30 skills, 8 status effects, 2 evolution lines (Fizzlet → Neonyx, Scrapup → Gearhound), with original procedural sprites at 16 px (overworld) and 48 px (battle).
- **Overworld Wildlings:** a population manager spawns by habitat tile, time of day and weight, out of the player's view, and despawns distant ones. Behaviour is personality-driven: shy species flee, curious ones approach, territorial ones charge, sleepy ones nap (walk up quietly for a first strike), social Ventwings move in flocks, and predators (Cindertail, Gearhound) chase prey that flees from them.
- **Turn-based battles** in the simulation (pure and seeded): speed and priority ordering, damage formula, STAB, crits, accuracy and stages, status ticks, recoil, healing, switching and forced switches, items, flee, XP sharing with an underdog bonus, level-ups, skill learning and evolution. Fainting causes **injuries** that lower max HP until a clinic visit.
- **Battle AI:** `wild` (weighted random favouring damage), `novice` (greedy with 20% noise) and `skilled` (greedy, heals under 30% HP with items, switches out of hopeless matchups to a counter).
- **Capture:** three Snare types (Tin, Coil with a Spark/Alloy bonus, Lull with a status bonus), with an HP- and status-dependent formula and 0–3 shake animations.
- **Party (6) and storage**, Wildling Index (seen/caught/lore), bag with overworld medicine, shops (Paper Moon Pawn, Glow Noodle), and a free clinic (Saint Ottilie) that heals and clears injuries. Blacking out sends you to the clinic and costs 10% of your cash.
- **Missions** (handcrafted, data-driven effects): *Last Run* (story opening: pickup → capsule hatches → Glitchling → Aegis trainer battle → clinic), *Binbandit Chase* (a quest Wildling flees through the alleys), *Vik's Challenge* (skilled rival, two Wildlings, uses items).
- **Local saves:** IndexedDB, 3 slots plus autosave (every 3 minutes and after missions), versioned envelope with a CRC-32 checksum, 2 rotating backups per slot with automatic recovery, a snapshot migration framework (v1 → v2 tested), and Continue / Load from the main menu.
- **UI:** battle overlay (Phaser stage with animations plus React menus), pause menu with Party / Bag / Index / Journal / Save / Controls, shop, toasts, HUD with objective, money and party HP. Everything works with keyboard, arrows and a gamepad.

## Critical review

- *Battle UI split across Phaser and React.* Accepted (ADR-010): React owns menus and text; Phaser only animates FX derived from the same log. The log is the single source of truth, so the two cannot disagree about outcomes.
- *Mission scripting as an effect DSL rather than code.* Accepted (ADR-012). It covered three different mission shapes without special cases. Branching choices arrive with dialogue trees in M5.
- *The overworld freezes during battles* (ADR-013). Simpler and fair. It means world time does not pass during a battle, which is acceptable.
- *The capture formula's floor of 2% at full HP* keeps "lucky first throws" possible without trivialising capture.
- Challenged and kept: **no PP system yet**. Fights are short, and the item economy is the resource pressure. Revisit with balancing in M7.
