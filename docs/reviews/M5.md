# Milestone 5 Progress Review — Story and Content (part 1)

> QA Engineer and Critical Reviewer · 2026-11-23 · Status: **the core campaign is playable from start to finish**. Breadth items remain (see "Remaining").

## Delivered (3 commits)

1. **City graph and Neon Downtown**:
   - a multi-district simulation: the active district is fully simulated, others are parked, and faction strategy keeps running abstractly;
   - travel at road exits, taking your car along; a Warden checkpoint keeps the bridge closed until *Last Run* is done;
   - Downtown itself: a strict grid of big blocks, glass-and-steel towers, eight landmarks, four new archetypes with routines, heavier patrols and new shops;
   - three new species (Wattbug, Hydrantoad, Heistcoon; Binbandit now evolves);
   - a Downtown music mood (F♯ Dorian), and waypoints that point across districts.
2. **Story campaign**:
   - branching choices gated by flags and reputation, saved with your game; missions gated by flags and held items;
   - **Act I:** *Last Run* → *The Hum* (scripted blackout, frenzied Neonyx) → *Everyone Wants It* (keep the Glitchling with the Saints, go independent, or sell it);
   - **Act II:** *Tethered* (Saints), *Recovered Asset* (Downtown, ally with or report Dr. Wren), and *Loyal Employee* for players who sold the Glitchling. Each yields a Hush Fragment;
   - **Act III:** *The Quiet*: boss battle with Corvin Ashgrave, then up to **four endings** (The Chorus, Lights Out, The Quiet, Varga's City), gated by your choices and alliances;
   - an **epilogue** assembled from world state (territory, faction standing, the Glitchling's fate, people you helped, Index, Wardens).

## Bugs found and fixed

- **Unreachable ending:** selling the Glitchling closed the Saints arc, leaving only one fragment, so the finale could never start. Fixed with the *Loyal Employee* route and fragment-count gating; the playthrough test covers it.
- Auto-start story missions were only rechecked when a mission completed, so a fragment gained through a dialogue choice didn't start the finale. Now they're also rechecked after every choice and hourly.
- A test helper shoved the conversation target out of reach (test-only bug).
- "▸" rendered as a comma in Press Start 2P inside the choice buttons (the same glyph issue as in M1).

## Critical review

- *A compact story before breadth* was deliberate: one complete, testable spine is worth more than five half-built arcs. The playthrough tests guard it against regressions as content grows.
- *Endings are gated, not scored.* Clear, readable conditions (e.g. The Chorus needs that you kept the Glitchling plus an ally inside Halcyon or Saints trust) beat hidden point totals.
- *The Glitchling can be sold* and the game stays completable; the player gets a "licensed" Scrapup so creature gameplay never soft-locks.
