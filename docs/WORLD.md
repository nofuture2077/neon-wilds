# NEON WILDS — World Design Document

> Owners: World Designer and Narrative Designer · Canon source for lore, factions, characters and story.
> Rule: any content (dialogue, missions, events) must be consistent with this document. Changes to canon go through the Game Director.

## 1. Vesper City

A coastal metropolis built on fog-bound marshland where Wildlings once lived in great numbers. Forty years ago the **Vesper Civic Trust**, the unelected board of old families that really runs the city, carried out *the Lighting*: a citywide neon grid sold as a modernisation and safety project. Crime dropped, business boomed, and the Wildlings drew back to the city's edges, drains and ruins. Nobody asked why.

**The secret:** the grid's ballast transformers emit the **Hush**, a sub-audible frequency that unsettles Wildlings and keeps the city-dwellers docile. Long-term exposure also makes *people* a little more compliant. The Trust has known for four decades.

**Tone:** rain, neon, humour and warmth among the poor, cold gloss among the rich. Wildlings are pests, pets, partners, products or prophets, depending on who you ask.

### 1.1 Recurring motifs

- **The hum:** neon tubes hum louder near the conspiracy. NPCs mention it, Wildlings react to it, and the soundscape uses it.
- **Halos:** the Gutter Saints' spray-painted halo; Halcyon's consumer *Halo* earpiece; the Hush's waveform. The same circle carries three meanings.
- **Couriers:** the city runs on couriers who carry what others will not, and they see everything.

## 2. Districts

Each district has a distinct visual palette, population, creature ecology, economy, faction balance and secrets. District access opens through story beats, faction standing or illegal routes.

### 2.1 The Slums *(starting district, Milestone 1)*

- **Look:** tar roofs and patched brick, laundry lines, dumpsters, potholes; pink, amber and teal neon; puddles everywhere.
- **Population:** night-shift workers, street vendors, kids, old-timers, scrap tinkerers, Gutter Saints runners, a few Warden patrols.
- **Wildlings:** Fizzlet, Cindertail, Puddlefin, Cracksprout, Binbandit; Neonyx at night.
- **Services:** Glow Noodle (food and buffs), Saint Ottilie Free Clinic (free healing, Saints-aligned), Paper Moon Pawn (sell items, cheap Snares, fence stolen goods), Kasimir's Cables & Coils (Spark gear), Rustbucket Garage (vehicle repair and resprays, M3), Mother Rook's Kitchen (Saints HQ front), Vesper Courier Co-op (courier job board).
- **Faction presence:** Gutter Saints (dominant), Halcyon Outreach Kiosk (infiltrating), Warden Substation 14.
- **Activities:** courier runs, Binbandit chases, street battles with kids, the vendor-protection racket.
- **Secrets:** the welded tunnel under Rook Avenue, which leads to Old Town; a sealed substation where the Hush is audible; Kit Ferro's "secret door" in the park.
- **Story role:** the opening act. The package hatches, and the Saints and Halcyon both want it.

### 2.2 Neon Downtown

- **Look:** glass towers, holo-billboards, clean wide boulevards, rooftop gardens; white, cyan and magenta.
- **Population:** office workers, socialites, Halcyon Aegis security, influencer-trainers with designer Wildlings, valets, buskers.
- **Wildlings:** Wattbug, Arcmoth, Ventwing, Spirewing, Hydrantoad, Heistcoon.
- **Services:** Halcyon Flagship Store (licensed Wildlings, premium Snares), Halcyon clinic (fast, expensive), the Prism Club (nightlife, informants), a car dealership.
- **Faction presence:** Halcyon Biotech (dominant), Vesper Civic Trust (hidden), Wardens HQ.
- **Activities:** high-end heists, the street racing circuit, Wildling beauty contests, corporate espionage.
- **Secrets:** the Trust's private floor in the Lumen Spire; the decommissioned first neon sign, "VESPER WELCOMES YOU", which still broadcasts the original Hush prototype.

### 2.3 Industrial Zone

- **Look:** smokestacks, sodium-orange light, rail yards, scrapyards, chemical pools.
- **Population:** factory workers, Iron Chain enforcers, scrappers, Halcyon lab techs, pit fighters.
- **Wildlings:** Smogling, Smogmaw, Scrapup, Gearhound, Scrapjaw, Pebblet, Slabbard, Kilnback, Pyrolynx, Tarslug variants.
- **Services:** the Furnace Pits (underground battle arena), scrap merchants (crafting), the Iron Chain union hall, the Halcyon Lab 7 perimeter.
- **Faction presence:** Iron Chain (dominant), Halcyon Biotech (Lab 7).
- **Activities:** pit-fight ladder, scrap salvage, sabotage, the labour-strike questline.
- **Secrets:** Lab 7's sub-level where Project LANTERN tethers Wildlings; a buried Kilnback colony keeping the old foundry warm.

### 2.4 Old Town

- **Look:** cobblestones, gas lamps, cathedral spires, collapsed arcades, ivy; dim amber and green; the least neon in the city.
- **Population:** antiquarians, Verdant Choir acolytes, tunnel guides, artists, the very old.
- **Wildlings:** Mossling, Ivyclad, Gloomkit, Nightprowl, Gargrin.
- **Services:** the Choir's chapel (status cures, Bloom gear), an antique shop (rare Technique Discs), the tunnel guides' guild.
- **Faction presence:** Verdant Choir (dominant), with contested pockets of the Tidewater Compact (smuggling tunnels).
- **Activities:** tunnel exploration, history puzzles, Shade-type night hunts.
- **Secrets:** the Founders' Vault, which holds the original Lighting blueprints with the Hush specification; a network of pre-Lighting Wildling murals that maps legendary habitats.

### 2.5 Harbor District

- **Look:** cranes, container stacks, fish markets, wet planks, fog horns; sickly green and orange.
- **Population:** dockers, fishmongers, smugglers, Compact crews, customs Wardens on the take.
- **Wildlings:** Brinekin, Dockhulk, Gutterjaw, Puddlefin, and Saltmaw (legend).
- **Services:** the Night Market (black-market Wildlings and illegal Snares, with dynamic prices), the shipwright (boats), the Compact's counting house.
- **Faction presence:** Tidewater Compact (dominant), Halcyon shipping office.
- **Activities:** smuggling runs (vans and boats), container heists, auction rigging.
- **Secrets:** the drowned ferry where Saltmaw sleeps; Compact ledgers that show Halcyon *buying* wild-caught Wildlings it claims to breed.

### 2.6 The Outskirts

- **Look:** abandoned suburbs, overgrown highways, a forest swallowing a theme park; almost no neon, so the stars are visible.
- **Population:** hermits, Choir pilgrims, rare-Wildling hunters, squatters, lost Halcyon field teams.
- **Wildlings:** Fernfox, Grovevix, Asphalm, Thicket species, Hollowstag (secret), and strong wild packs.
- **Services:** a hermit trader (barter only), a Choir waystation.
- **Faction presence:** Verdant Choir (pilgrim camps); Halcyon (field capture teams).
- **Activities:** expeditions, rare captures, survival at night (Wildlings are bolder beyond the Hush).
- **Secrets:** the Hollow, an ancient grove where Hollowstag can be met (secret ending); the old marsh lighthouse, the Trust's backup Hush transmitter.

## 3. Factions

The relationship matrix below is the starting state. It shifts through faction AI and player actions.

|  | Saints | Halcyon | Compact | Choir | Iron Chain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gutter Saints** | — | Hostile | Wary | Friendly | Neutral |
| **Halcyon Biotech** | Hostile | — | Business | Hostile | Business |
| **Tidewater Compact** | Wary | Business | — | Wary | Friendly |
| **Verdant Choir** | Friendly | Hostile | Wary | — | Hostile |
| **Iron Chain** | Neutral | Business | Friendly | Hostile | — |

### 3.1 The Gutter Saints

- **Philosophy:** *Nobody's coming to save us, so we save each other.* A mutual-aid street gang that runs soup kitchens, clinics and protection, and keeps its own justice.
- **On Wildlings:** family. Strays are fed, injured ones healed, and trafficking is punished.
- **Leadership:** **Mother Rook** (Odile Rook, 61, ex-nurse, warm and terrifying). Lieutenants: **Dez Okafor** (enforcer, loyal, hot-headed) and **Needle** (medic and fixer). Runners do the street work.
- **Territory:** the Slums; footholds in the Harbor.
- **Economy:** protection dues (low), clinic donations, salvage, a cut of the Night Market from Saints sympathisers.
- **Strategic goals:** keep Halcyon out of the Slums; protect strays; find out why Wildlings are disappearing.
- **Distinctive missions:** rescue tethered Wildlings, defend vendors, smuggle medicine, expose the Outreach Kiosk.
- **Inner conflict:** Dez wants open war with Halcyon, while Rook wants proof first. The player can tip that balance.

### 3.2 Halcyon Biotech

- **Philosophy:** *Progress is a gentler city.* Licensed breeding, Wildling healthcare and consumer neurotech (the Halo earpiece).
- **On Wildlings:** a resource and intellectual property: domesticated, licensed, improved.
- **Leadership:** CEO **Ilse Varga** (48, brilliant and pragmatic; she believes LANTERN will end Wildling attacks). Head of R&D **Dr. Tobias Wren** (guilt-ridden; a possible informant). Aegis security chief **Mara Quell** (loyal to the money).
- **Territory:** Downtown; Lab 7 in Industrial; outreach kiosks spreading through the Slums.
- **Economy:** licensed Wildling sales, clinics, Halo devices, Trust contracts.
- **Strategic goals:** finish Project LANTERN, recover the escaped prototype (your Glitchling), expand into the Slums.
- **Distinctive missions:** corporate espionage, field-capture contracts, "recover company property" (that is, betray your friends).
- **Twist:** Varga is being *used*. The Trust funds LANTERN to upgrade the Hush into the Quiet. She can become a key ally if shown the proof.

### 3.3 The Tidewater Compact

- **Philosophy:** *Everything floats if the price is right.* Smugglers' guild with a strict code: debts are paid, and the crew comes first.
- **On Wildlings:** cargo and currency, but rare ones are respected the way a sailor respects the sea.
- **Leadership:** **Captain Oriel Danso** (55, charming, ledger-minded, never forgets a debt). Quartermaster **Fen Halloway**, auctioneer **"Gilt" Marlo**.
- **Territory:** the Harbor; Old Town tunnels; boats.
- **Economy:** the Night Market, smuggling routes and fencing.
- **Strategic goals:** control the tunnels, keep Halcyon as a client and not a master, find Saltmaw.
- **Distinctive missions:** smuggling runs, auction heists, debt collection, navigating Warden checkpoints.

### 3.4 The Verdant Choir

- **Philosophy:** *The city was theirs first.* An eco-mystic movement that hears the Hush and believes the Wildlings are the city's rightful heirs.
- **On Wildlings:** sacred. Capture is acceptable only as guardianship; trading is sin.
- **Leadership:** **The Cantor** (Seraphine Vale, 39, ex-Halcyon acoustician, charismatic, increasingly radical). **Brother Lark** (gentle and doubting). **Thorn** (militant cell leader).
- **Territory:** Old Town; Outskirts pilgrim camps.
- **Economy:** donations, herbal medicine, a little sabotage-for-hire.
- **Strategic goals:** shut down the neon grid. Thorn's cell plans to blow up the substations, which would hurt people.
- **Distinctive missions:** sabotage, pilgrim escort, decoding the murals, stopping (or helping) Thorn.

### 3.5 The Iron Chain

- **Philosophy:** *Strength is the only honest currency.* Once the dock and foundry workers' union; now it runs the city's underground pit-fighting circuit too.
- **On Wildlings:** fighters and partners. Respect is earned in the pit. Abuse is frowned on, but tolerated for a price.
- **Leadership:** **"Foreman" Brannoc Hale** (50s, huge, honourable by his own rules). Pitmaster **Ines "Ledger" Sarr**. Champion **Vik Torrance**, the player's rival trainer.
- **Territory:** the Industrial Zone.
- **Economy:** pit betting, labour contracts, scrap.
- **Strategic goals:** protect jobs from Halcyon automation, keep the pits independent, and rival the Compact for the docks.
- **Distinctive missions:** pit ladder, strike-breaking or strike-supporting, rigged fights, Vik's rivalry arc.

### 3.6 Vesper City Wardens *(institutional, not a competing faction)*

The police force with its **Wildling Control Unit (WCU)**. Commissioner **Adaeze Kord** is honest but boxed in by the Trust; **Sergeant Pell** is corrupt and on the Compact's payroll. The Wardens run the wanted system and keep a "Law" standing with the player.

### 3.7 The Vesper Civic Trust *(hidden antagonist organisation)*

The old families behind the Lighting. Chairman **Corvin Ashgrave** is the central antagonist: a public philanthropist, patient, sincere in believing that control is kindness. Members are embedded in every faction. Each faction has one *Trust asset* the player can expose, such as Halcyon's Mara Quell and the Wardens' Sergeant Pell.

## 5. Main story

> **Implemented (M5 part 1):** Act I in full; Act II with the Saints and Halcyon arcs (+ *Loyal Employee* for the sold path); Act III needs **two** fragments for now, and four of the five endings are implemented (The Hollow needs the Outskirts). Content lives in `packages/sim/src/content/missions.ts`.

### Act I — *The Package* (Slums)

1. **Last Run.** A Co-op courier job: deliver a sealed capsule from the Harbor to the Halcyon kiosk. Aegis agents ambush the drop, the capsule cracks, and the **Glitchling** imprints on you.
2. **Everyone Wants It.** The Saints shelter you (Dez, Needle). Halcyon posts a "recover company property" bounty, and the Compact offers to buy it.
3. **The Hum.** Bram Kestrel shows you the frequency readings. The Glitchling shorts a substation and the Wildlings in the block go wild for a night. **First decision:** hand the Glitchling to Halcyon for a fortune (a short, bleak path that ends Act I differently and closes some routes), keep it with the Saints, or go independent.

### Act II — *Five Doors* (open structure)

Investigate LANTERN through **any three of five faction arcs**, in any order. Each arc yields one **Hush Fragment** (part of the blueprint), a faction relationship result and a district change:

- **Saints:** rescue tethered strays from a Halcyon van (Slums → Industrial).
- **Halcyon:** infiltrate Lab 7 as a "recovered asset handler"; turn Dr. Wren.
- **Compact:** win the Night Market auction for a crate of tethered Wildlings and trace the ledgers.
- **Choir:** decode the Old Town murals and the Founders' Vault, and contain (or join) Thorn's sabotage.
- **Iron Chain:** climb the pit ladder to fight a tethered Scrapjaw, and learn the pits test LANTERN fighters.

**Midpoint, "The Trust":** the fragments reveal the Hush and the Quiet. Ashgrave contacts the player personally with a generous offer and a gentle warning.

### Act III — *The Quiet* (citywide)

The Trust triggers the upgrade early. Tethered Wildlings turn on districts and Halo users go blank. Factions you allied with (relationship ≥ Trusted and their arc completed) send help in the finale. Factions you burned may fight you or each other. Final assault: the Lumen Spire (Downtown) or the marsh lighthouse (Outskirts, if found).

### Endings (at least three, plus a secret)

| Ending | Requirements | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **The Chorus** *(Accord)* | 3+ allied factions; Glitchling friendship max; Varga or Wren turned | The grid is retuned to a harmony, and humans and Wildlings share the city. Districts gain mixed habitats; faction balance follows your alliances |
| **Lights Out** | Destroy the grid (Choir path, or with no allies) | The neon dies and Wildlings reclaim the streets. A free but dangerous city; the Choir rises; the Slums suffer most at first |
| **The Quiet** | Accept Ashgrave's offer or seize the grid yourself | Order. Wildlings are tethered and citizens docile. You run the city from the Spire; your friends remember |
| **Varga's City** | Give LANTERN control to Halcyon after exposing the Trust | Corporate stewardship: safer, expensive, unequal |
| **The Hollow** *(secret)* | All 8 murals, Hollowstag befriended, Glitchling evolved | Hollowstag sings the original marsh song; the Hush breaks gently and the forest returns to the Outskirts |

The epilogue is a slideshow per district and per main character, assembled from world state: faction territory, NPC memory (who you helped or hurt) and key choices.

## 6. Side content

- **Faction questlines:** 6–8 missions per faction beyond the Act II arc, ending in a faction rank-5 finale.
- **Character arcs:** Vik's rivalry (5 battles, from enemy to friend or bitter foe); Juno's courage (protect her or not); Bram's experiments; the Wren confession.
- **Procedural activities:** see GDD §9 and AI.md §8.
- **World events:** rain floods (Tide surge), blackouts (Spark frenzy, crime spike), Halcyon recall drives, pit tournaments, Night Market auctions, Choir processions, Warden crackdowns, faction raids.
- **Secrets:** 8 murals, 12 hidden Wildling nests, tunnel maps, the "VESPER WELCOMES YOU" sign, Kit's secret door, Pell's ledger.

## 7. Dialogue style guide

- Short lines (at most about 110 characters per page), 1–4 pages per conversation.
- Every recurring NPC has a **voice sheet**: vocabulary, rhythm, a verbal tic, and what they never say.
- Conversations are **branching trees** with conditions on memory, relationships, faction standing, time and story flags (DOMAIN_MODEL.md §Dialogue).
- Barks are one-liners per context: ambient, night, shoved, alarmed, witnessed crime, greeting a friend, threatened.
- `{player}` inserts the player's name. Never break the fourth wall; tutorials are told in-world by the Co-op dispatcher.
